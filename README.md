# Aegis Nexus

An AI-powered global intelligence and situational awareness platform.

## Overview

Aegis Nexus is an experimental intelligence system designed to collect, analyze, and interpret information from multiple sources in real time. Its goal is to identify important developments, detect emerging patterns, and provide structured insights about world events, technology, economics, and other rapidly changing domains.

Rather than acting as a simple news aggregator, Aegis Nexus attempts to understand relationships between events, evaluate significance, and surface information that may require attention.

## Features

*  Real-time information monitoring
*  AI-powered event analysis
*  Trend and pattern detection
*  Rapid intelligence summaries
*  Cross-source correlation
*  Emerging event tracking
*  Situational awareness dashboard
*  Automated insight generation

## Vision

The long-term goal of Aegis Nexus is to function as a personal intelligence layer that helps users understand what is happening, why it matters, and what could happen next.

Instead of information overload, the objective is information clarity.
